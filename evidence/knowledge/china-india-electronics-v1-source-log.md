# China-to-India electronics knowledge graph: official-source verification log

Checked: 2026-08-26 (Asia/Kolkata)

Scope: Wave 1 / Task Chat 2 research for a domain-general China-to-India electronics path. This log records only current first-party Government of India sources that were re-opened during this task. It is not an admission record and does not establish product-specific applicability. Admissions must still be produced by the opt-in live capture harness through `admitSourceEvidence`.

## Admission boundary

- A portal or authority page can support its own name, URL, access state, applicant category, and expressly listed procedure.
- It cannot establish that an arbitrary electronics item is in an ITC-HS entry, BIS/QCO schedule, MTCTE Essential Requirement, or WPC licence-exempt frequency band.
- Product descriptions are not applicability evidence. Exact technical characteristics and the current official statutory entry are both required.
- Where the official page is public but the transaction is behind login, only the public page metadata is verifiable here. No filing, account state, fee payment, submission, or clearance was checked.
- Fees, deadlines, filer roles, sequence, and document lists remain Pending unless the cited locator expressly supports the exact field.
