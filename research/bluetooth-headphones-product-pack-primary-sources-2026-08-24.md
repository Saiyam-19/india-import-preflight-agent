## 1. Exact admitted boundary

This pack admits only a **new, finished, single-model, retail-packaged over-ear Bluetooth headphone set for resale in India** with all of these facts fixed:

- the left and right sound channels are physically joined by a headband and are not True Wireless Stereo (TWS);
- wireless audio is Bluetooth only in the licence-exempt 2400–2483.5 MHz band;
- there is no wired audio input and no Wi-Fi, cellular, satellite, NFC or other radio;
- the product has an integrated microphone and one integrated rechargeable lithium-ion portable secondary battery whose exact manufacturer, model and capacity are known;
- charging is through a passive USB cable only; no external charger, power bank or charging case is included;
- the movement is an ordinary foreign import, not an SEZ-manufactured-goods clearance into the Domestic Tariff Area;
- the imported article is a finished retail product, not an input for manufacturing microphones, hearables, semiconductors or other equipment;
- the exact headphone model, manufacturer, importer, country of origin, producer and exporter are known; and
- the goods are new, finished and presented as one retail sales unit, not parts, components, a bundle, used goods or refurbished goods.

Outside this pack are TWS/earbuds/in-ear devices, neckbands, wired-only or hybrid wired-audio headphones, hearing devices, Bluetooth speakers, products with another radio, products with a removable or separately supplied battery, products supplied with a charger/power bank/charging case, parts, components, multi-product bundles, used/refurbished goods and any product with an unknown or ambiguous model or supply configuration.

## 2. Deterministic tariff mapping and import policy

The admitted facts map to **85183019 — headphones and earphones capable of connecting through wireless medium: Other**.

Primary official support:

- CBIC Customs Tariff, Chapter 85, printed p.1019 / PDF p.16, lines for `8518 30 11`, `8518 30 19`, `8518 30 20` and `8518 30 90: <https://www.cbic.gov.in/content/pdf/CONTENTREPO/Customs/Tariff/Tariff(ason30.06.2024)/CUSTOMS_TARIFF_VOL-I/chap-85.pdf>. The text separates TWS whose sound channels are not connected by wire (`85183011`), other wireless headphones (`85183019`) and wired-only headphones (`85183020`).
- Live ICEGATE description/import-policy response for `85183019`, checked 2026-08-24: <https://www.icegate.gov.in/Webappl/Desc_details?cth=85183019&item_desc=>. It returns `85183019`, description `Other`, import policy `Free`, and unit `kg`.

Why adjacent codes do not apply:

- `85183011` is excluded because the admitted left/right sound channels are joined through the headband rather than being TWS.
- `85183020` is excluded because the admitted device is Bluetooth-capable and has no wired audio input.
- `85183090` is not selected because the tariff supplies the more specific wireless `Other` item.

Any change to those distinguishing facts is a new classification question and must return `Needs verification` without a cost.

## 4. Rules, evidence, clearance effects and remediation

### 4.1 WPC Equipment Type Approval — customs condition and legal-readiness blocker

- DoT/WPC ETA service page, checked 2026-08-24: <https://eservices.dot.gov.in/equipment-type-approval-eta>. The page expressly lists headphones/earphones, requires ETA for import/sale/use of de-licensed wireless equipment, specifies RF report/manufacturer authorisation/technical literature and says the declaration may be used for Customs clearance.
- WPC ETA FAQ, Q2, Q5, Q8 and Q10: <https://eservices.dot.gov.in/sites/default/files/faqs/eta_faq.pdf>. These require exact-finished-product ETA, RF reports for all modules and the import undertaking used with Customs.
- DoT import compendium, PDF p.7 paras 12.1–12.2 and Annexure III pp.9–10: <https://eservices.dot.gov.in/sites/default/files/circular-notifications/Compendium%20of%20Orders%20related%20import%20licence%20-signed%20copy%20060722.pdf>.
- Licence-exempt 2.4 GHz rules, G.S.R.45(E), official compendium PDF pp.141–144, especially English rules 3–6: <https://www.dot.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/84f33f09e137fa81930f44bcd5f2d238.pdf>.

Required evidence: exact-model ETA, accredited RF report for the single Bluetooth radio/band, manufacturer authorisation/technical literature, and the signed/system-generated import undertaking. Missing or mismatched evidence blocks Customs clearance and legal readiness. Remediation is to obtain/correct the exact-product ETA and supporting documents; never reuse another model’s ETA.

### 4.2 BIS registration for the finished wireless headphone — customs condition and legal-readiness blocker

- Electronics and IT Goods compulsory-registration amendment S.O.1100(E), dated 2020-04-01, Gazette PDF p.4 S.No.51 “Wireless Headphone and Earphone”; paragraph 3 makes the added category effective six months after publication: <https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/MeitY-QCO-for-additional-12-products.pdf>.
- Current BIS Scheme II list, checked 2026-08-24, S.No.51: <https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-ii-registration-scheme/?lang=en>.
- S.O.4997(E), dated 2025-10-29 and published 2025-11-04, English clauses (ii) and (v), PDF p.3: <https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Migration-to-IS-IEC-62368-Part-1-2023-from-IS-13252-Part-1-2010-and-IS-616-2017.pdf>. It replaces IS 616:2017 with IS/IEC 62368-1:2023 and permits concurrent use until 2028-11-01.
- DGFT General Notes Regarding Import Policy 2025, paragraph 2.03(c), PDF p.2: <https://content.dgft.gov.in/Website/General_Notes_to_Import_Policy_2025.pdf>. Notified electronic/IT goods that are not BIS-registered and label-compliant are prohibited imports and must be re-exported or rendered unusable/scrapped if they reach Customs.

Required evidence: valid exact-model/family BIS registration and standard mark/registration number under the permitted transition standard. Missing, expired, wrong-model or label-mismatched evidence blocks Customs clearance and legal readiness. Remediation is exact-model registration/marking or re-export/destruction under the official import note, not substitution of a related model certificate.
