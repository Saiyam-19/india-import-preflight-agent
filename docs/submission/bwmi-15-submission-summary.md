# India Trade Compliance Assistant - submission summary

Word count: 218

India Trade Compliance Assistant helps an importer prepare a commercial China-to-India or India-to-China shipment without pretending to replace Customs, a licensed broker, or a government portal.

The first screen is chat. A user can describe an unfamiliar electronics product in ordinary language, attach supporting documents, and receive one action dossier. The first reply asks for quantity, value, origin and destination, product evidence, purchase stage, proof of purchase, and purpose. Already-purchased goods switch to clearance and remediation guidance.

The dossier presents documents, classification and regulatory checks, exact policy paragraphs and pages, verified forms, official contacts, duties and costs, blockers, owners, and government submission portals. A portal appears only when its exact service URL is admitted. The dossier then states the documents uploaded there, filer, access and login requirements, fee, deadline, and sequence. Missing fields remain Pending. The app never requests credentials, signs in, uploads, pays, submits, or claims clearance.

One focused OpenAI Agents SDK agent can run optional deep research when configured. Server-owned tools control evidence admission, arithmetic, citation validation, case isolation, and readiness. Deterministic instant guidance remains available without a provider. Uploaded PDF, JPEG, and PNG bytes are discarded after bounded extraction; reviewable derived facts and bounded document metadata persist locally.

This release package proves the local build and bilateral India-China scope. Public deployment is intentionally not claimed.
