# Preserve original-language authority

The Regulatory Knowledge Corpus will preserve and cite the original Chinese, Arabic, English, or other official text as the Authoritative Text, together with its exact locator and content hash. Official English publications may be stored as parallel authoritative versions; otherwise English text is a labelled Derived Translation, and any Translation Ambiguity material to applicability, duty, or action becomes an Unresolved Evidence Gap that prevents a positive Compliance Assessment.
