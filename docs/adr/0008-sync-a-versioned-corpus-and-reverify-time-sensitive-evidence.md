# Sync a versioned corpus and reverify time-sensitive evidence

Primary official documents will be preserved as content-addressed versions through an explicit Corpus Sync command, while Live Official Research may add Provisional Evidence without a background daemon. Time-Sensitive Evidence such as tariffs, sanctions, trade remedies, restrictions, and exchange rates must be reverified during every assessment; retrieval failures and unresolved amendment chains remain visible evidence gaps rather than being replaced with cached assumptions.
